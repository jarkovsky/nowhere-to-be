How to play

Arrow keys or WASD to drive. Space to brake. R to return home. H for a friendly horn. B opens the scrapbook and C opens car choices.

For tilt steering, hold your phone or tablet like a wheel, turn on Tilt steering, and allow motion access. Use Center steering to adjust your grip. Tilt needs an HTTPS game link.

Little explorer is gentle cruise control. You steer; the car drives itself. Hold brake to stop.

Discoveries quietly appear in your scrapbook. To stay at camp, open this menu when you’re beside the campsite.
